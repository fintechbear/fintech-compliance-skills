# P06 — refund/cancel, retention/erasure, financial PR review

**Status: PASS** (validated commit `7bd85a8`).

P06 adds three NARROW, independently-triggerable workflow entries (no single mega-skill),
each referencing the control catalog by FCS id, with runnable real-PostgreSQL references
for the two that have deterministic invariants.

## What was added

### Controls (+5 → 23 total)
- **FCS-REFUND-001** — a refund is bounded by its source capture; running sum of refunds
  never exceeds the captured amount; partials accumulate; concurrent refunds serialized.
- **FCS-REFUNDTERM-001** — cancel/void (before capture) vs reversal/refund (after capture)
  are distinct, provider-specific operations, not interchangeable synonyms.
- **FCS-RETAIN-001** — retention is decided per data category by legal basis: mandatory
  financial retention vs active legal hold vs erasable profile data. No blanket rule.
- **FCS-ERASE-001** — over-retention AND over-deletion are both failures, checked
  separately; crypto-shredding is a technique, not a universal legal erasure guarantee.
- **FCS-PRREVIEW-001** — per-finding review (control id + file/line + trigger + evidence +
  confidence + review limits); reviews the changed risk surface; no unverified finding as
  fact; no automatic numeric compliance score.

Refund proofs: full/partial refunds; cap rejection; same-key replay (no double refund);
**5 concurrent 300-refunds against a 1000 capture → exactly 3 confirm, total 900 ≤ cap**
(serialized under `SELECT … FOR UPDATE`); unknown provider outcome stays `pending_unknown`
(not failed, not re-sent) and still counts against the cap; cancel/void refused after
capture and refund refused after void (lifecycle, not synonyms); cross-tenant refund
rejected.

Retention proofs: profile data erased while a mandatory financial record is kept; legal
hold blocks deletion of otherwise-erasable data; an expired mandatory record becomes
erasable; an unknown category is not blindly deleted; a retention sweep removes
over-retained data without over-deleting mandatory-in-window data; over-retention and
over-deletion measured separately; crypto-shred records a method string, never claimed as
universal legal erasure.

## Honesty notes / blockers
- The provider is a labelled **test double**, not a real sandbox (by design).
- Retention **durations are DEMO values**, not cited regulation; the category-by-legal-basis
  reasoning is the control, not the numbers.
- `fintech-pr-review` is a **discipline**, deliberately without a deterministic harness; its
  qualitative judgement is a real-agent / NEEDS_REVIEW item deferred to P08, not auto-passed.
- A post-suite `docker run` client hang (host was saturated by the user's parallel P16-P17
  acceptance job) delayed clean exit; the `timeout 300` wrapper would have cleared it, and
  the run's 49/49 evidence was already written before cleanup. CI has no docker-in-docker
  (postgres is a service), so this cannot occur there.
